A way of seeing

I am a director and filmmaker interested in everything that can turn an image into a story. I work on audiovisual, branded, and cinematic projects, moving between directing, shooting, and editing. I want to build images that have an identity, a rhythm, and something to tell, rather than images that only show.

Cinema and the audiovisual are the center of my work, but my eye also comes from an interest in the history of contemporary art and in video games as a medium.
